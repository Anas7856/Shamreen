Meet The Artist

A Voice Rooted In Culture.

Sharmeen Osmany is a Pakistani singer and recording artist whose music draws inspiration from the rich heritage of Pakistani folk and Sufi traditions.

Her musical journey began in August 2016 with the release of her debut song, Hamarri Sanson Mein. The song gained recognition across digital platforms, FM radio and music television channels, introducing her distinctive musical expression to a wider audience.

With mentions in more than 80 music blogs worldwide, her work has reached listeners across different countries, connecting audiences through the universal language of music.

Inspired by her cultural roots, Sharmeen explores the sounds of Pakistani folk, Sufi pop fusion and experimental music. Her artistic approach brings together traditional influences and contemporary arrangements, creating a musical identity that is both rooted in heritage and open to new possibilities.

Through her music, she celebrates the richness of Pakistani musical traditions while expressing her own creative individuality.

A celebration of heritage, emotion and the art of music.
